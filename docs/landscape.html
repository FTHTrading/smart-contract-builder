🔗 Chainlink Stack

Chainlink has evolved far beyond price feeds into a comprehensive middleware layer for tokenized RWAs. Their stack now covers data, valuation, solvency, cross-chain movement, identity, and orchestration. Every module below is a hook Smart Contract Builder contracts can integrate with.

Data Feeds · Data Streams · SmartData

Chainlink Integrated (RWAOracle)

Decentralized oracle networks providing tamper-proof market data. Data Feeds for standard rate/price data (SOFR, LIBOR, gold spot, FX). Data Streams for high-frequency sub-second updates needed for complex trading products. SmartData embeds Net Asset Value, Assets Under Management, and reserve status directly into a token's metadata so it carries its valuation wherever it moves.

Our integrationRWAOracle.sol — AggregatorV3 wrapper with 4 safety checks (non-negative, complete round, staleness, heartbeat)
Reference deploymentsCMBS waterfall reads Chainlink SOFR feed for floating-rate accrual · REIT distributor reads NAV feed via SmartData for share price

Proof of Reserve

Chainlink Integrated (PoR Consumer)

Replaces opaque, manual off-chain audits with continuous, cryptographic verification. Monitors off-chain vaults (fiat, gold, real-estate deeds) via APIs and pushes updates on-chain based on deviation or heartbeat thresholds. Wired directly into token mint paths as an automated circuit breaker: if reserves fall below circulating supply, minting auto-pauses.

Our integrationProofOfReserveConsumer.sol — reads PoR feed, drives circuit breaker over mint with configurable over-collateralization ratio
Live PoR users21.co (ARK 21Shares BTC ETF) · Backed · Bedrock · Wenia/Bancolombia

Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP)

Chainlink Integrated (CCIP Bridge)

Universal messaging + transfer layer across 70+ blockchains. Burn-and-mint mechanism means no honey-pot liquidity pool to drain — supply stays unified. Programmable transfers can bundle tokens + data + instructions atomically (send stablecoins, pass compliance check, mint credit shares, route yield back, all in one transaction).

Our integrationCCIPBridgedToken.sol — ERC-20 with burn-and-mint cross-chain bridging via peer-whitelisted CCIP router
Institutional pilotsSwift + UBS (tokenized fund subscriptions) · ANZ (DvP under Project Guardian) · SBI Digital + ADDX (cross-chain fund admin) · HSBC + Ant Group (cross-border tokenized deposits)

ACE + DECO — Identity & Privacy

Chainlink Integration Design

ACE (Automated Compliance Engine) — routes verified off-chain KYC/AML data to on-chain contracts. "Write Once, Read Many" reusable identity: users verify once with a trusted provider, receive a portable cryptographic credential, any whitelisted dApp can instantly query it. Plug-and-play with ERC-3643.

DECO — Zero-Knowledge Proof oracle. Investors cryptographically prove facts ("I am an accredited investor") from a web session without exposing the underlying PII to oracle nodes or the public ledger.

Our integration pathOur PermissionedToken already uses the ERC-3643-inspired transfer-hook shape ACE plugs into. Design contract: replace the direct IdentityRegistry.eligibleFor() call with an ACE-attested claim check.
Why this mattersOnboarding friction is the single largest cost driver for institutional RWA adoption. "Write Once, Read Many" cuts per-issuance investor-verification cost from ~$500 to under $10.

Chainlink Runtime Environment (CRE)

Chainlink Integration Design

All-in-one orchestration layer that composes Data Feeds (pricing) + Proof of Reserve (solvency) + ACE (identity) + CCIP (settlement) into unified workflows. Effectively "the Chainlink stack as one API." Reduces the deployment complexity of a full RWA workflow from 5+ separate integrations to one CRE workflow definition.

Where this fitsThe CRE is a natural upstream orchestrator for Unykorn Studio's plan executor. A single CRE workflow could produce the Studio approvals for a full RWA issuance, with our audit-trail receipts capturing the human-authorization events at each stage.

🏦 Tokenized Private Credit Market Leaders

Private credit is the largest single segment of the tokenized RWA market. As of 2026, five platforms dominate — each with a distinct borrower segment, blockchain choice, and operational model.

Figure Technology Solutions

HELOCs + Consumer Loans $18–$20.1B represented

Undisputed market leader by total represented value. Built on the Provenance Blockchain. Dominance driven by high-volume consumer lending (HELOCs, mortgage transactions). Blockchain-native Loan Origination System (LOS) + Digital Asset Registry Technology (DART) compress origination cost to ~$1,000/loan and time-to-close from weeks to days.

MarketplaceFigure Connect — packages loans, distributes to institutional buyers · 56% of Q1 2026 tokenized loan volumes
Recent innovationYLDS — first SEC-registered, yield-bearing stablecoin offered as a public security, backed by prime money market securities
Institutional partnerApollo — successfully executed and transferred mortgage transaction ownership on-chain
Shape matchOur RealEstateDebtToken ships the same fungible ERC-20 claim on a single mortgage/HELOC pattern Figure pioneered on Provenance — servicer-recorded P+I payments, accumulator distribution, note + deed hash pinning.

Maple Finance

Institutional Lending Pools $4.6–$5B AUM · $2B active loans

Largest institutional lending venue in DeFi. Curated credit marketplace managed by "Pool Delegates" (BlockTower, Room40, AQRU) who underwrite borrowers, negotiate fixed rates, and post first-loss capital. Cumulative loans since 2022 launch: $22B+. Targeting $100B annual loan volume by 2030.

Loan structure (post-2022 restructuring)Corporate loans overcollateralized at 105–130% in liquid assets (BTC/ETH/SOL). 30–180 day fixed-rate terms. Pool Delegates take first-loss tranche.
Retail bridgesyrupUSDC / syrupUSDG permissionless tokens package institutional yield for retail — syrupUSDG deployed on Robinhood Chain for Robinhood Earn
Warehouse facilityOn-chain warehouse for Kraken's OTC lending desk (2026)
Cash managementVault holding tokenized US Treasuries (BlackRock BUIDL, Circle USYC) for DAO/crypto-firm treasuries
Named Pool DelegatesBlockTower Capital (structured credit backed by receivables + equipment), Room40 Ventures, AQRU. Each posts 2-10% of pool NAV as first-loss capital. See integrations map for per-firm breakdown.
Our integrationPoolDelegatePool.sol ships the same shape: whitelisted lenders, Pool Delegate role, first-loss capital tranche, per-borrower concentration caps, cooldown-gated redemptions

Centrifuge

Trade Finance + Structured Credit ~$430M active pools + $880M Janus JTRSY

Longest-running dedicated private credit marketplace. Focuses on securitizing off-chain assets (trade finance, invoices, consumer credit) into senior and junior tranches. Purpose-built for bespoke, structured credit pools that compound more slowly than broader institutional funds.

Janus Henderson partnershipHosts $880.7M tokenized Treasury fund (JTRSY)
Sky (formerly Maker) integrationCentrifuge debt tranches used as collateral in Sky protocol — originators borrow USDS stablecoin against real-world assets. Historically funded 85%+ of Centrifuge-originated loans. Concrete example: MakerDAO provided $150M in senior capital backing BlockTower's $220M Centrifuge origination in Oct 2022 — the deal that established the "originator-retains-junior + Sky-funds-senior" template. See integrations map for the mechanism.
Named originatorsBlockTower Capital (holds junior "TIN" tranche, absorbs first loss, largest single institutional originator by AUM). Other pools originated by various asset managers across trade finance, invoices, consumer credit, real-estate bridge loans.
Shape match — three families Our library ships the full Centrifuge product surface across three contracts:
  • InvoiceFactoringPool — short-tenor receivables (30-90 days), the Tinlake origination shape
  • BridgeLoanTranchePool — ongoing origination of bridge loans, two tranches (originator retains junior first-loss, on-chain investors buy senior), senior tranche freely transferable so it composes with Sky protocol for USDS-collateralized borrowing
  • InventoryFinancingSPV — single-asset asset-backed loan for physical inventory (Kevan's $2M EV charger reference deal in Tennessee), UCC-1 lien hash pinned on-chain, senior/junior tranche stack with amortizing waterfall

Goldfinch

Emerging-Market Debt Low tens of millions active

Prominent platform focused on emerging market debt and non-bank lenders. Early pioneer whose active loan value declined from 2021–2022 peak following borrower defaults. Recently pivoted to a highly structured institutional wrapper called Goldfinch Prime.

Institutional wrapper stackSecuritize as regulatory issuance layer · Anchorage Digital Bank as qualified custodian

RealT · Lofty · Fractional Property Equity

Retail Property Tokenization $100M+ combined property NAV

Reference shape for tokenizing the equity side of real estate — a Delaware LLC legally owns a single property, membership interests are issued as fractional ERC-20 shares, and net rental income (after property tax, insurance, maintenance reserves) is deposited into the contract at operational cadence (daily for RealT, monthly for larger commercial). Holders claim pro-rata via accumulator pattern. On property sale, sponsor deposits sale proceeds and holders redeem tokens for cash. Regulatory posture: RealT restricts to non-U.S. retail via Reg S; other issuers use Reg D 506(c) for U.S. accredited. Set required claim topics accordingly.

Portfolio compositionPrimarily U.S. Midwest residential single-family properties. Sub-$150K NAV per property makes retail-scale fractional ownership economically viable.
Distribution cadenceRealT: daily USDC distributions from rental income. Lofty: weekly. Larger commercial issuers: monthly.
Shape matchOur FractionalPropertyToken ships this exact shape — sponsor mints during offering, property manager deposits net income, accumulator distributes to holders, sale-of-property redemption mechanism retires the token. Property address hash + deed hash + SPV formation doc hash pinned on-chain for evidentiary provenance without exposing the physical address.

Securitize

SEC-Registered Transfer Agent ~$3.5B tokenized assets across all classes

General RWA platform (not credit-specific). SEC-registered transfer agent and issuance platform for major institutional funds including Apollo's tokenized credit fund and BlackRock's BUIDL. Routes tokenized equities, Treasuries, and credit — effectively the compliance backbone for institutional issuance.

Notable issuancesBlackRock BUIDL (tokenized Treasury fund) · Apollo tokenized credit fund · Goldfinch Prime wrapper
Shape matchOur PermissionedToken ships the same ERC-3643-shape investor eligibility layer Securitize uses for its transfer-agent role. Deploy in parallel with a Securitize-registered offering.

📍 Where Unykorn fits

Everyone above is a peer or an integration surface, not a competitor. Our position is specific and defensible:

  • Substrate, not product. Figure, Maple, Centrifuge, Securitize each own a segment. We ship the audit-trail infrastructure that any of them (or any new entrant) can use to make their issuances independently verifiable by counsel.
  • Chainlink integration, not replacement. Every Chainlink module — Data Feeds, PoR, CCIP, ACE, DECO, CRE — becomes a step kind in Unykorn Studio's executor, with its own receipt action in the ledger. We don't rebuild any of it; we make it approvable and auditable.
  • Open library, not walled garden. Every contract in this repo is MIT-licensed. Every integration pattern in Studio is documented. Sponsors who use Studio for one deal own their audit ledger (a plain JSONL file); Unykorn doesn't lock them in.
  • The seam nobody else covers. Foundry produces artifacts. OpenZeppelin Defender administers post-deploy. Tokeny enforces transfer rules. None of them produce an unbroken cryptographic chain from "the operator asked for this" through "this exact bytecode was compiled from these sources" through "this draw released against this waiver." That's the whole product.
